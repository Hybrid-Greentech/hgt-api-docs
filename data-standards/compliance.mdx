## Bound assessment

The platform assesses compliance per asset and per metric over a rolling one-hour window. Both of the following must hold for every bound on [Market requirements](/data-standards/markets):

- At least **99%** of intervals are within the bound.
- **No single gap** exceeds **10 times** the bound.

The percentile absorbs ordinary jitter and retransmission; the maximum gap catches a silence short enough to hide inside it.

The same pair applies to delivery lag. For roundtrip response, at least 99% of activations in the window must be answered within the bound.

### Events

Report alarms, faults, and non-operational states on the events channel, classified by `domain` and `severity`:

```
{priceZone}/{customerName}/events/{assetId}
```

Report a communication failure or an asset that has gone non-operational rather than going silent. The platform cannot distinguish a silent gap from an asset that stopped reporting.

Task lifecycle does not belong on this channel. Publish `STARTED`, `COMPLETED`, `FAILED`, `INVALID`, and `UNAUTHORISED` as task events instead. See [Events](/delegated-operators/operational-flow#events) for both payloads.

### Prequalification

Before go-live, an asset must meet the requirements for its markets for **24 continuous hours** in the sandbox environment.

An existing integration takes on any newly added obligation at its next prequalification, and stays compliant under the previous requirements until then.

## Value integrity

Publish sampled values, never derived ones. Do not:

- **Average, smooth, or decimate.** If the on-site value changes faster than the data frequency, publish the change. Do not publish a rolling mean in place of samples.
- **Interpolate.** Do not generate intermediate values between samples.
- **Re-stamp a cached reading.** Do not apply a new `assetTimestamp` to a value the site sampled earlier.

<Warning>
  A re-stamped cached value is indistinguishable from a real sample at the integration boundary, so the platform does not detect it in the stream. Hybrid Greentech verifies value integrity at prequalification and during incident investigation.
</Warning>

## Timestamps

Every measurement message carries two timestamps:

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| `assetTimestamp` | When the site sampled the value |
| `serverTimestamp` | When Hybrid Greentech received the message |

The platform assesses data frequency on the gap between consecutive `assetTimestamp` values, and delivery lag as `serverTimestamp − assetTimestamp`. It assesses each separately.

## Clocks

Your clock sets `assetTimestamp` and the platform's sets `serverTimestamp`, so clock offset counts toward delivery lag.

| Requirement | Value |
|-------------|-------|
| Time source | NTP or better, within 50 ms of UTC |
| Correction method | Slew, never step |
| `assetTimestamp` ordering | Strictly increasing per asset and metric |

An `assetTimestamp` later than its `serverTimestamp` indicates a clock fault on site.

## Transport

| Channel | Direction | QoS |
|---------|-----------|-----|
| `measurements` | Operator to platform | 0 |
| `events` | Operator to platform | 1 |
| `tasks/events` | Operator to platform | 1 |
| `tasks` | Platform to operator | 1 |
| `activation` | Platform to operator | 1 |

Measurements use QoS 0 because a sample redelivered by a broker retry has already breached its delivery lag and cannot be used for control or settlement. Events and task events are low-rate and individually meaningful, so the platform delivers them at least once.

The `measurements` channel has two additional requirements:

- **Do not publish retained messages.** A retained power value appears live to any new subscriber.
- **Use a clean session.** A reconnecting site must not deliver a backlog of samples that are already outside the delivery lag bound.

### Stale activations

Activations are delivered at QoS 1, so after a reconnection the broker can deliver a setpoint that is several seconds old. Discard an activation if either of the following is true:

- Its `timestamp` is older on receipt than the roundtrip bound for the asset's market.
- A newer activation for the same task has already been applied.

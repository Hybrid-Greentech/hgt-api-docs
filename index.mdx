Hybrid Greentech provides access to all flexibility markets and energy markets. Building on Hybrid Greentech's platform, you can take any controllable asset, prequalify them and monetize the availability as flexibility.

## Getting started with the integration

To begin with the integration, you must get in contact with your Account Manager at Hybrid Greentech. The public APIs discussed here are focused on dispatch, auditability, and measurements as a part of the operations model. Reporting APIs are available as a part of our dashboard platform and can be seen as a part of a demo.

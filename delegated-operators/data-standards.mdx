## Scope

This page defines how often a site reports data and how quickly it responds to setpoints. If another page or diagram states a different rate, use the values on this page.

The requirements apply to every asset integrated through the Delegated Operators API, whatever the hardware is. Scope follows the integration path rather than the equipment type, so a combined battery and EV charger integrated through this API is in scope.

Other integration paths have their own requirements:

| Integration | Requirements |
|-------------|--------------|
| Direct PPC and OEM hardware over industrial protocols | [Signal List](/controller/signal-list) |
| EV chargers integrated through the EVSE API | [EV Chargers](/distributed-resources/welcome) |
| Heat pumps integrated through the heat pump API | [Heat Pumps](/distributed-resources/welcome) |

You are responsible for meeting these requirements. Hybrid Greentech verifies them at prequalification and when investigating an operational incident. The platform does not publish a compliance feed, so the absence of a warning is not confirmation that a site is compliant.

<Note>
  These requirements apply to all integrations, including existing ones. Pages that previously described a flat 1-second rate were incorrect.
</Note>

## Timestamps and bounds

Every measurement message carries two timestamps:

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| `assetTimestamp` | When the site sampled the value |
| `serverTimestamp` | When Hybrid Greentech received the message |

Requirements are expressed as two bounds over these timestamps. The platform assesses each bound separately.

### Sample interval

The **sample interval** is the time between the `assetTimestamp` of one measurement and the `assetTimestamp` of the next measurement of the same type for the same asset.

Publish a sample on every interval, including when the value has not changed. A value identical to the previous one is still a sample. The platform treats a gap in the stream as a fault, not as an unchanged value.

Publishing faster than required is always acceptable. The sample interval is a maximum gap, not a target rate.

### Delivery lag

The **delivery lag** is `serverTimestamp − assetTimestamp`. It must be 250 ms or less for every measurement type and every asset class.

The same bound applies whether the site connects to the broker directly (cloud-to-site) or through your own cloud (cloud-to-cloud). The bound includes clock offset, so the site's time source must meet the accuracy in [Clocks](#clocks).

### Value integrity

Both bounds assume that published values are sampled rather than derived. Do not:

- **Average, smooth, or decimate.** If the on-site value changes faster than the sample interval, publish the change. Do not publish a rolling mean in place of samples.
- **Interpolate.** Do not generate intermediate values between samples.
- **Re-stamp a cached reading.** Do not apply a new `assetTimestamp` to a value the site sampled earlier.

<Warning>
  A re-stamped cached value looks the same as a real sample at the integration boundary, so the platform does not detect it in the stream. Hybrid Greentech verifies value integrity at prequalification and during incident investigation.
</Warning>

## Requirements by asset class

Requirements are keyed on asset class, the `kind` field of the asset specification returned by `GET /v2/assets`. They do not depend on the market product the asset is delivering: a battery trading only day-ahead publishes at the same rate as one delivering aFRR, because publish rates are set in edge configuration rather than per trade.

| Measurement type | BESS (`bess`) | PV (`pv`) | When required |
|------------------|---------------|-----------|---------------|
| `powerAc` | 100 ms | 1 s | Always |
| `frequency` | 100 ms | 100 ms | When the site measures grid frequency |
| `stateOfCharge` | 5 s | n/a | Always, for BESS |
| `afrrBaseline` | 1 s | 1 s | When the asset is enrolled in aFRR |
| `powerDc` | 1 s | 1 s | Optional, diagnostic only |
| `powerSocManagement` | n/a | n/a | Deprecated, see [Deprecated measurement types](#deprecated-measurement-types) |

All values are sample intervals. The delivery lag bound is 250 ms in every case.

If an asset's `kind` is not listed, apply the BESS values until Hybrid Greentech publishes requirements for that class. Contact your account manager during onboarding to confirm the requirements that apply.

## Grid frequency

Grid frequency is a property of the connection point rather than of an individual asset, so the requirement is the same for every asset class: a 100 ms sample interval, including for PV.

Publish frequency when the site has its own frequency measurement. A site without a frequency device is not expected to provide one.

The measurements channel is addressed per asset, so each asset publishes the frequency that its own controller measures, on its own topic:

```
{priceZone}/{customerName}/measurements/{assetId}
```

A site with more than one asset therefore publishes the frequency value more than once. This is intentional. For frequency-responsive products, the platform pairs an asset's power response with the frequency the same controller measured on the same clock.

## aFRR baseline

`afrrBaseline` has a 1 s sample interval for both asset classes. The baseline is a sum of commanded setpoints rather than a physical measurement:

```typescript
afrrBaseline = FCRD + FCRN + FFR + mFRR + Wholesale
```

The value is piecewise-constant, so holding the last value between samples is expected.

Publish a baseline measurement immediately whenever any constituent setpoint changes, without waiting for the next scheduled sample. A setpoint step that lands between samples otherwise misattributes up to one second of energy to aFRR.

<Warning>
  Do not include the aFRR setpoint in the baseline. The platform derives delivered aFRR energy by subtracting the baseline from measured power.
</Warning>

## Setpoint response time

An activation carries a setpoint to an asset during an active task. The **setpoint response time** is the time from the platform publishing that activation to the platform receiving a measurement that shows the asset moving toward the commanded value.

| Asset class | Setpoint response time |
|-------------|------------------------|
| BESS (`bess`) | 1 s |
| PV (`pv`) | 5 s |

PV has the longer bound because a plant controller distributes the setpoint across an inverter fleet.

The bound covers command delivery and the start of the response. It does not require the ramp to be complete: an asset that begins responding within the bound and reaches the target over the following seconds meets this requirement. Full activation times are set by the market product and confirmed at prequalification.

There is no acknowledgement message. The platform detects the response in the measurement stream that the site already publishes.

The bound leaves the following time for the site's own decision and actuation:

| Component | BESS | PV |
|-----------|------|-----|
| Sample interval of the responding measurement | up to 100 ms | up to 1 s |
| Delivery lag | up to 250 ms | up to 250 ms |
| Available for site decision and actuation | ~650 ms | ~3.75 s |

At least 99% of activations in a rolling one-hour window must be answered within the bound.

### Discard stale activations

Activations are delivered at QoS 1, so after a reconnection the broker can deliver a setpoint that is several seconds old. Discard an activation if either of the following is true:

- Its `timestamp` is older on receipt than the setpoint response time for the asset class.
- A newer activation for the same task has already been applied.

## Clocks

`assetTimestamp` is set by the site's clock and `serverTimestamp` by the platform's, so clock offset counts toward the delivery lag.

Each site must meet the following:

| Requirement | Value |
|-------------|-------|
| Time source | NTP or better |
| `assetTimestamp` ordering | Strictly increasing per asset and measurement type |

An `assetTimestamp` later than its `serverTimestamp` indicates a clock fault on site.

## Transport

| Channel | Direction | QoS |
|---------|-----------|-----|
| `measurements` | Operator to platform | 0 |
| `events` | Operator to platform | 1 |
| `tasks/events` | Operator to platform | 1 |
| `tasks` | Platform to operator | 1 |
| `activation` | Platform to operator | 1 |

Measurements use QoS 0 because a sample redelivered by a broker retry has already breached the delivery lag and cannot be used for control or settlement. Events and task events are low-rate and individually meaningful, so they are delivered at least once.

The `measurements` channel has two additional requirements:

- **Do not publish retained messages.** A retained power value appears live to any new subscriber.
- **Use a clean session.** A reconnecting site must not deliver a backlog of samples that are already outside the delivery lag bound.

Publish one measurement per message. The `measurementMessage` schema carries a single `measurementType` and a single value, and the platform does not accept batched payloads.

A BESS enrolled in aFRR publishes approximately 21 messages per second per asset: `powerAc` and `frequency` at 10 Hz, `afrrBaseline` at 1 Hz, and `stateOfCharge` every 5 s.

## Compliance

The platform assesses compliance per asset and per measurement type over a rolling one-hour window. Both of the following must hold:

- At least 99% of intervals are within the bound.
- No single gap exceeds 10 times the bound.

| Bound | 99% within | Maximum single gap |
|-------|------------|--------------------|
| 100 ms sample interval | 100 ms | 1 s |
| 1 s sample interval | 1 s | 10 s |
| 5 s sample interval | 5 s | 50 s |
| 250 ms delivery lag | 250 ms | 2.5 s |

The percentile absorbs ordinary jitter and retransmission. The maximum gap catches an outage short enough to stay inside the percentile: 30 seconds of silence is 0.8% of a one-hour window.

### Declared outages

Gaps inside a declared outage are excluded from the percentile and remain in the record. Declare an outage by publishing to the events channel with `domain: NETWORKING` or `domain: NOTAVAILABLE`:

```
{priceZone}/{customerName}/events/{assetId}
```

Declare communication failures rather than leaving a silent gap. The platform cannot distinguish an undeclared gap from an asset that stopped reporting.

### Prequalification

Before go-live, an asset must meet these requirements for 24 continuous hours in the sandbox environment.
